# ShopSphere — Business Questions

## Business Problem

ShopSphere is an Indian e-commerce company experiencing growth in sales but facing uncertainty around customer retention, product profitability, and increasing product returns.

The objective of this analytics project is to transform raw e-commerce data into actionable business insights that can support better decisions around customers, products, pricing, retention, and operations.

---

## Core Business Questions

### Sales Performance
1. How much revenue and profit does ShopSphere generate?
2. How are sales changing over time?
3. Which categories contribute most to revenue and profit?

### Product Performance
4. Which products generate the highest revenue?
5. Which products generate the highest profit?
6. Which products have high sales but low profitability?

### Customer Intelligence
7. Who are the most valuable customers?
8. How frequently do customers purchase?
9. What is the average customer order value?
10. Which customers are at risk of becoming inactive?

### Customer Retention
11. What percentage of customers make repeat purchases?
12. How does customer retention change over time?
13. Which customer cohorts have the strongest retention?

### Returns & Customer Experience
14. Which products and categories have the highest return rates?
15. What are the most common return reasons?
16. Is customer rating related to product returns?

### Pricing & Discounts
17. Do discounts increase order volume?
18. Do high discounts reduce profit margins?
19. Which categories are most sensitive to discounts?

### Geographic Performance
20. Which cities generate the highest revenue?
21. Which cities have the highest average order value?
22. Which regions have high customer growth but low revenue?

### Product Relationships
23. Which products are frequently purchased together?
24. Which products could be recommended as cross-sell opportunities?

---

## Final Objective

The final solution will combine:

- Python
- Pandas
- SQL
- PostgreSQL
- Power BI
- Statistical analysis
- Customer segmentation
- Cohort analysis
- Market basket analysis

to create an end-to-end e-commerce analytics and customer intelligence platform.
